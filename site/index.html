Beyond the IPL

IPL squad players scatter across the world between seasons — internationals, the Big Bash, county cricket, tri-series nobody televises. This follows every player on an IPL 2026 roster, everywhere else they turn out.

    Ball-by-ball data from Cricsheet (CC BY 4.0), aggregated into per-innings figures. Squads are transcribed from public listings and pinned to Cricsheet player identifiers by hand, so a player is followed across competitions by identity rather than by name.
